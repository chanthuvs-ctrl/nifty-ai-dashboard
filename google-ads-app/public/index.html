How to Connect Your Google Ads Account

1. Get Your Customer ID

Log into your Google Ads account at ads.google.com. Copy the 10-digit number in the top right corner (e.g. 123-456-7890).

2. Developer Token (API Access)

In Google Ads Manager Account, go to Tools & Settings → API Center and copy your Developer Token.

3. Alternative: 1-Click Google Ads Script

If you don't have an approved Developer Token yet, you can copy our built-in Google Ads Script into your account (Tools → Scripts) to automatically push spend & search terms into this dashboard without API approval delays!
